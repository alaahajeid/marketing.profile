01 / Financial Services

Building a bank brand around something bigger than advertising: trust.

Al Rajhi Bank Jordan — Marketing & Brand Transformation

Working across campaign strategy, partnerships, content, activations, customer communication and brand experiences — with a focus on making financial-services marketing more approachable and relevant.

TRUST / EVERY TOUCHPOINT
TRUST

not a campaign line.
the operating idea.

Context

Financial-services marketing asks more than product promotion. Customers are making decisions around money, security and long-term relationships.

The thinking

Strong communication is only one layer. Trust is built through consistency across the message, the experience and the small details customers remember.

What I do

Campaign strategy, partnerships, content, activations, customer communication and brand experiences, working with stakeholders to move from idea to execution.

What I learned

A simple idea or positive customer experience can leave a deeper mark than a large advertising budget. In banking, every touchpoint is part of the brand.

02 / Partnerships + Education

Taking financial education onto campus.

Universities Project — INGOT · January 2024–May 2025

A nationwide university initiative combining educational seminars, demo trading competitions, activations and partnerships to give students practical financial knowledge and market experience.

JUPSUTGJUHTUPetra
The challenge

Turn financial education into something practical, engaging and relevant to university students — while building partnerships that could last beyond a single activation.

My role

Concept development, partner outreach, university coordination, execution, media coverage and performance reporting, with collaboration across legal/compliance and marketing teams.

The experience

Seminars, demo trading competitions and campus activations created multiple ways for students to learn, participate and engage directly.

Outcome

The initiative generated thousands of student engagements and helped develop long-term university relationships. No unpublished performance figures are used here.
